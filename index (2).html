Our Idea

Why we started making pasta in the first place.

"We didn't want to invent a supplement. We wanted to fix a pasta."

01

The gap

Every high-protein pasta we tried in the fitness-oriented Zug crowd we're building for had the same problem: solid macros, forgettable taste. We noticed a lot of people just went back to regular pasta and tracked around it.

02

The program

Through Youth Enterprise Switzerland (YES), five of us got the structure to actually build a company around this instead of just complaining about it: a founding contract, a real budget, and a deadline to ship something.

03

The recipe

We went through round after round of test batches, comparing texture and bite against regular pasta, not just against other protein brands, until it stopped tasting like a compromise.

04

The pitch

Strongnoodles is our answer for the 18 to 35 crowd around Zug who train seriously and are done pretending a chalky pasta is a personality trait.
